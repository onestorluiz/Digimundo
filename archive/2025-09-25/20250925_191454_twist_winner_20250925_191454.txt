1. SHYAMALAN'S REVERSAL - HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT & EMOTIONAL REVERSAL: Marcus's true identity as a vigilante is gradually revealed through clues such as his childhood journal, turning the "good cop" persona into something darker. His character journey is inverted as he goes from trying to be good like his mother wanted to becoming a vigilante taking justice into his own hands.

2. MCKEE'S REVERSAL - REVERSAL OF FORTUNE & TURNING POINT: Marcus's reversal of fortune occurs when he is exposed as the killer, and Sarah turns from trusting him to arresting him. The turning point is when Sarah realizes that Marcus has been causing the deaths of the priests, leading to this reversal.

3. TRUBY'S REVEAL - STUNNING REVEAL & SELF-REVELATION: Marcus's true identity as a vigilante is the stunning reveal, changing our understanding of his character and actions throughout the story. His self-revelation occurs when he admits that he made a promise to stop the abuse at St. Mary's and has been working towards this goal for 37 years.

4. ARISTOTLE'S PERIPETEIA - PERIPETEIA & ANAGNORISIS: The peripeteia is Marcus's exposure as a vigilante, which reverses his fortune from appearing to be a trustworthy cop to being a criminal. The anagnorisis occurs when Sarah and the audience realize the truth about Marcus's actions and motivations.

5. HITCHCOCK'S SURPRISE - SURPRISE VS SUSPENSE: The surprise is the reveal of Marcus as a vigilante, which is unexpected and changes our understanding of the story. There is also suspense as we wonder if Marcus will be caught and what consequences he will face for his actions.

6. FINCHER'S UNRELIABLE - UNRELIABLE NARRATOR & REALITY QUESTIONING: While not explicitly an unreliable narrator, Marcus's true identity as a vigilante challenges our assumptions about him and forces us to question the reality of the story up until this point. The careful placement of clues also adds to the sense of reality questioning, as we realize that Marcus has been manipulating events all along.

7. NOLAN'S TEMPORAL - NESTED REALITY: While not a traditional nested reality, the complex plot structure of Marcus's vigilante actions and Father O'Brien's involvement adds layers to the story that are gradually revealed, creating a sense of depth and complexity.

8. CHRISTIE'S DETECTIVE - EVERYONE GUILTY: The reveal of Marcus as a vigilante implicates not only him but also Father O'Brien, Commissioner Reynolds, and the entire system of corruption within the Church. This creates a sense of everyone being guilty to some extent, as they have all contributed to or turned a blind eye to the abuse at St. Mary's.

9. SERLING'S TWILIGHT - IRONIC REVERSAL: The ironic reversal is that Marcus's quest for justice leads him down a dark path, ultimately resulting in his own downfall and exposure as a criminal. His desire to stop the abuse at St. Mary's becomes a curse that he cannot escape, even after achieving his goal.

10. USUAL SUSPECTS TECHNIQUE - KEYSER SOZE REVEAL: The reveal of Marcus as the vigilante is similar to the Keyser Soze reveal in Usual Suspects, as both involve a character who has been manipulating events all along and whose true identity is only revealed at the end. There are also visual confirmations, such as the journal entries and Father O'Brien holding a gun, that add to the sense of revelation and confirmation.
