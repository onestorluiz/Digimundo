1. **Refusal of the Call** - Marcus is a former priest who has distanced himself from his religious vocation, showing his initial resistance to fully accepting his role as a protector of justice. (Campbell p.59)

2. **Threshold Guardian** - Elena, who was once a nun, serves as a guardian at the threshold of Marcus's decision to uphold or abandon justice. She tests his resolve by challenging his actions and reminding him of their shared past oath to preserve life. (Vogler p.57)

3. **Belly of the Whale** - Marcus faces his deepest internal conflict as he grapples with the morality of his decision, weighing justice against his religious beliefs and personal sense of righteousness. (Campbell p.90)

4. **Ordeal** - The central crisis occurs when Marcus must confront the consequences of his actions and their impact on his soul and relationship with Elena, ultimately deciding whether to continue down this path or seek redemption. (Vogler p.155)

5. **Atonement** - Conflict arises between Marcus and ultimate religious authority as he questions God's role in justice, leading him to challenge the concept of forgiveness within his faith. (Campbell p.126)

Applying Campbell's specific techniques:
- Marcus embodies both hero and villain by taking justice into his own hands while wrestling with moral dilemmas (p.136).
- The confession note symbolizes the temptation of "the meeting with the goddess/temptress" as it presents an opportunity for forgiveness but also incites further conflict (p.109).
- Marcus's conflict with religious authority reflects the "father as ogre-tyrant" pattern, challenging God's role in justice and morality (p.129).
- Through confronting his internal dragons, Marcus transcends his original beliefs and undergoes an apotheosis towards a higher understanding of justice and faith (p.149).

Applying Vogler's specific techniques:
- Elena serves as both a mentor and ally to Marcus while also acting as a threshold guardian, creating a complex conflict web (p.135).
- The approach to the Morrison boy's "inmost cave" - his confession note - builds tension by hinting at the dark consequences of Marcus's actions (p.143).
- Through this experience, Marcus undergoes a death and rebirth, shedding his old beliefs and emerging with newfound understanding (p.159).
- The script ends on an ambiguous note, implying that Marcus may be able to return with the "elixir" of knowledge to resolve his internal conflicts and heal his relationship with Elena (p.193).
