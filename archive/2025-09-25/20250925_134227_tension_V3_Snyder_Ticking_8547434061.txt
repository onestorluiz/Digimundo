1. **THE TICKING CLOCK PRINCIPLE:**
- Immediate: Marcus called Elena, indicating an urgent need for help and confession.
- Psychological: Marcus is deteriorating both physically (shaking hands) and spiritually, which can have long-term consequences if not addressed soon.
- Moral: The moral dilemma Marcus faces with the administration of justice creates a ticking clock for Elena to intervene before he crosses a point of no return.
- Legal: The impending discovery of the truth by authorities is a constant threat, adding another layer of urgency.
