1. Central Dramatic Question: The central dramatic question in this script is, "Will Marcus lose his soul for seeking justice?" This question sets up the main source of tension as Marcus struggles with his desire for understanding and absolution while facing the obstacle of Elena's moral stance.

2. Desire vs Obstacle: Marcus desires understanding and absolution (desire), but is met with Elena's moral opposition (obstacle). This creates tension throughout the scene, as they both have valid perspectives yet are fundamentally at odds with one another.

3. Revelation Sequence Creating Tension:
   - First revelation: Marcus killed someone (confession note)
   - Second revelation: It was the Morrison boy (Elena's reaction)
   - Third revelation: Through communion wine with an added substance (Marcus' explanation)

These revelations build tension by revealing more information about Marcus' actions, forcing Elena and the audience to reevaluate their stance on the situation.

4. Opposition Escalation:
   - Level 1: Marcus vs himself (guilt)
   - Level 2: Marcus vs Elena (moral opposition)
   - Level 3: Marcus vs divine law (playing God)
   - Level 4: Marcus vs human law (murder)

The escalating opposition creates exponential tension, as Marcus' moral dilemma intensifies from internal conflict to external confrontation with both divine and human laws.

5. Moral Dilemma as Tension Source: The central moral argument of justice vs law, divine vs human authority, and mercy vs punishment drives the tension in this scene. Marcus believes he has acted justly, while Elena argues that he has overstepped his boundaries and violated sacred oaths.

6. Subplot Weaving for Tension: The subplot of lost faith is woven throughout the conversation between Marcus and Elena, adding tension through contrast. Both have lost their faith yet take different paths in dealing with it, further emphasizing their moral differences.

7. Desire Line Maintaining Tension:
   - Marcus's desire: Understanding → Absolution → Peace
   - Elena's desire: Help friend → Uphold morality → Save soul

The contrasting desires of Marcus and Elena create sustained tension, as their separate but intertwined goals are incompatible and lead to an emotional climax.

In summary, this script uses Truby's dramatic question and revelation techniques effectively to build tension through a moral dilemma, desire vs obstacle, escalating opposition, and conflicting desire lines. The carefully constructed revelations, as well as the subplot of lost faith, add layers of complexity to Marcus and Elena's relationship and the story's central question.
