# ARISTOTLE - POETICS - DICTION
Chapters 6 & 19 (1450b, 1456a)

## Core Principle
"Speech reveals character choice" (1450b)

## THOUGHT THROUGH SPEECH (1450b)
- Character reasoning revealed through words
- Logic of character exposed in dialogue
- Speech patterns show thinking process
- Words reveal decision-making

## RHETORIC IN DIALOGUE (1456a)
- Persuasive speech techniques
- Characters argue their position
- Logical construction of arguments
- Emotional appeals through words

## DICTION AS DRAMATIC ELEMENT
- One of six elements of tragedy
- Word choice reveals social status
- Language appropriate to character
- Speech must fit dramatic situation
