# SYD FIELD - SCREENPLAY - FUNCTIONAL DIALOGUE
Chapter 12: Dialogue (pages 213-220)

## Core Principle
"Dialogue must move story forward" (p.213)
"Good dialogue illuminates character and moves story" (p.214)

## EXPOSITION THROUGH CONFLICT (p.215)
- Hide exposition in argument
- Information delivered through tension
- Never have characters explain plot
- Bury facts in emotional exchanges

## CHARACTER-SPECIFIC VOICE (p.219)
- Dialogue reveals profession/background
- Each character has unique vocabulary
- Speech patterns show education level
- Dialect/slang reveals origins

## AUTHENTIC SPEECH PATTERNS (p.220)
- Real but not realistic
- Compressed naturalism
- Remove boring parts of real speech
- Keep rhythm of authentic conversation
