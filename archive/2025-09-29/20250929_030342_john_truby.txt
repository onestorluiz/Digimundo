# JOHN TRUBY - ANATOMY OF STORY - MORAL DIALOGUE
Chapter 10: Dialogue (pages 416-424)

## Core Principle
"Great dialogue comes from character, not cleverness" (p.416)
"Dialogue is moral action" (p.419)

## MORAL DIALOGUE (p.418)
- Values expressed through speech patterns
- Each character's moral code shapes their words
- Dialogue reveals ethical stance
- Speech patterns reflect worldview

## DIALOGUE REVEALS CHARACTER (p.420)
- Philosophy emerges through speech
- Word choice reveals education/background
- Rhythm shows emotional state
- Vocabulary exposes character's true nature

## VERBAL BATTLE (p.422)
- Best dialogue is verbal fighting
- Characters compete for moral high ground
- Each seeks to prove their truth
- Conflict drives every exchange

## DIALOGUE THROUGH SILENCE (p.424)
- Strategic withholding of speech
- Silence as power move
- What character refuses to say
- Pauses that reveal more than words
