# ROBERT MCKEE - STORY - DIALOGUE MASTERY
Chapter 17: "The Text" (pages 388-395)

## Core Principle
"Dialogue is not conversation" (p.388)
"In life we speak on the nose, in drama we speak in subtext" (p.390)

## SUBTEXT (p.389-392)
- The "text beneath text"
- What characters DON'T say matters more
- True meaning lies between the lines
- Characters say one thing but mean another

## DIALOGUE AS ACTION (p.393)
- Dialogue changes relationships
- Each line shifts power dynamics
- Words as dramatic beats
- Dialogue must alter the scene's balance

## DIALOGUE AS AMMUNITION (p.395)
- Characters use words as weapons
- Verbal attacks and defenses
- Strategic deployment of information
- Words to wound or heal

## SILENT DIALOGUE
- Communication without words
- Pauses carry meaning
- What's NOT said speaks volumes
- Silence as dramatic tool
