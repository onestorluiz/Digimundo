## ARISTOTLE'S POETICS:

Aristotle outlines the beginning-middle-end structure fundamentals, unity of action, plot as the soul of tragedy, reversal, recognition, and six elements of tragedy. Quoting from Poetics: "Tragedy is an imitation of an action that is serious, complete, and of a certain magnitude" (Aristotle 2). The confession note in the script indicates both a beginning (conflict establishment) and a middle (rising tension), while Marcus's internal conflict represents the unity of action. Reversal and recognition can be observed when Elena discovers the truth about the Morrison boy, leading to Marcus's self-realization of his actions.

## ROBERT MCKEE'S STORY:

McKee emphasizes three-level structure (Act, Sequence, Scene), story spine, inciting incident placement, progressive complications, crisis, climax, and resolution. He states, "Structure is a selection of events; the precise art of shaping time" (McKee 165). In this script, Marcus's internal struggle can be considered as Act One with his confession serving as the inciting incident. Progressive complications unfold through Elena's involvement and confrontation with Marcus, culminating in a climactic moment where Marcus justifies his actions.

## JOHN TRUBY'S ANATOMY OF STORY:

Truby introduces a 22-step story structure, focusing on seven key steps as the structural foundation. The structural elements present include weakness/need setup (Marcus's loss of faith), desire line (seeking justice for the murdered children), and self-revelation (realizing his actions contradict his oath). Truby argues that "organic structure is not a formula; it's an understanding of human nature" (Truby 183). This script demonstrates organic structure through Marcus's emotional journey.

## SYD FIELD'S SCREENPLAY:

Field presents the three-act structure paradigm, plot points, page count mathematics, and timing. Applying this methodology to the script reveals a simple yet effective structure with two major plot points (Marcus receiving the confession note and Elena's involvement) as structural hinges. The midpoint is observed in Marcus's internal debate regarding his faith and justice.

## CHRISTOPHER VOGLER'S WRITER'S JOURNEY:

Vogler introduces a 12-stage hero's journey structure, including ordinary world vs special world framework, threshold crossings as structural markers, and return with elixir. Marcus's apartment represents the ordinary world, while his confession note symbolizes the call to adventure. Crossing into Elena's world (special world) marks a significant threshold, and Marcus's struggle with faith serves as an internal journey before returning to his ordinary world.

## BLAKE SNYDER'S SAVE THE CAT:

Snyder outlines a 15-beat structure using openings/finals, catalyst timing, the B-story function, and finale structural payoff. In this script, the opening image shows Marcus alone with his internal conflict, while the final image suggests potential redemption or further struggle. The catalyst occurs when Marcus calls Elena for help, introducing a B-story (Elena's perspective on faith).

## JOSEPH CAMPBELL'S HERO WITH A THOUSAND FACES:

Campbell focuses on the monomyth circular structure pattern, 17 stages of the journey, and structural universality. The script demonstrates a simplified version of this mythic structure through Marcus's struggle for justice, confrontation with Elena, and self-realization.
