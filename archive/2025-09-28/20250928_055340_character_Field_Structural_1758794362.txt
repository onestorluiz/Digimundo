1. SETUP - WHO IS THIS CHARACTER? (300 words)

Marcus, a priest who has lost his faith, is introduced in the context of an abandoned church, his professional life as a clergyman seemingly abandoned along with it. His dramatic need, what drives him through the story, is to reconcile his actions and restore his faith. Marcus's point of view is one of despair and disillusionment, willing to go against the moral code of his profession. The change or growth he undergoes will be a transformation from a lost man to someone who can find solace in redemption.
