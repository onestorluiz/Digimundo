1. PRIMARY ARCHETYPE:

- Identify: The primary archetype here is the SHADOW, embodied by MARCUS.
- FUNCTION: The Shadow serves as an antagonist or a disruptive force within the protagonist's journey.
- MASK: Marcus, once a priest, dons the mask of religious devotion and moral authority but hides his darker impulses beneath it.
- Vogler states, "Archetypes are amazingly constant throughout all times and cultures" - The Shadow archetype represents the energy of the dark side that we often hide from others and ourselves.
