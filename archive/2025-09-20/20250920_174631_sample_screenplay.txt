FADE IN:

ACT I

EXT. CITY - DAY

JOHN walks through the busy streets.

SARAH (O.S.)
John! Wait up!

John turns. SARAH approaches.

SARAH (CONT'D)
We need to talk.

JOHN
About what?

SARAH
About the case. I found new evidence.

INT. COFFEE SHOP - LATER

John and Sarah sit across from each other.

JOHN
What kind of evidence?

SARAH
The kind that changes everything.

FADE OUT.
