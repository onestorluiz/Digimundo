c. Inconsistent pacing: Certain scenes progress too quickly, hindering the audience's ability to fully grasp the significance of events or relationships. Seger argues that a satisfying resolution must provide closure while also acknowledging the lasting effects of past experiences. This strategy will enhance the payoff when they finally meet and ensure that the audience remains engaged throughout the story.
d. Reinforce resolution: Revise the climax of the story to better reflect Samantha's journey and the complexities of her healing process. Instead of offering a simplistic solution, incorporate elements of struggle and perseverance that highlight her growth and determination. By doing so, the conclusion will resonate more deeply with the audience and reinforce the themes explored throughout the screenplay.
