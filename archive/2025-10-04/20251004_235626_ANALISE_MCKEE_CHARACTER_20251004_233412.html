First Paragraph: One recurring pattern detected by Python involves instances where characters explain their feelings directly instead of demonstrating them through behavior. As McKee explains, "on-the-nose dialogue... undermines the artistry of filmmaking" (McKee, p. 92). To address this issue, focus on incorporating subtext into dialogues and showing emotional states through physical actions.
Second Paragraph: Another pattern concerns the lack of depth in some characters, which affects the overall coherence of the story. McKee argues that "a well-crafted story world has its own internal logic" (McKee, p. 125), meaning that every element, including characters, should contribute to the consistency and richness of the narrative universe. Developing secondary characters further can help create a more immersive and engaging story world.
3. Problems

Problem 1: On-the-Nose Dialogue
Description: Characters frequently declare their emotions explicitly, which weakens the impact of their interactions.
Example: Samantha saying, "Mas eu estava tendo um sonho lindo..." after being awakened abruptly.
Impact: Such dialogue hinders the audience's ability to connect with the characters and understand their motivations.
Problem 2: Lack of Character Depth
Description: Some characters appear flat or inconsistent due to insufficient exploration of their backgrounds and personalities.
Example: Alberto's sudden appearance in the scene without any introduction or explanation of his relationship with Samantha.
Impact: A shallow understanding of characters makes it difficult for the audience to invest emotionally in the story.
Problem 3: Overuse of Exposition
Description: The screenplay relies heavily on exposition, often using dialogue to convey essential information rather than integrating it seamlessly into the narrative.
Example: The extensive use of voiceover narration in the opening scenes.
Impact: Excessive exposition disrupts the natural flow of the story, making it feel artificial and unengaging.
4. Solutions

Solution for Problem 1: Rewrite the dialogue to incorporate subtext and avoid direct expressions of emotion. For example, have Samantha turn away from Alberto, pulling the covers over her head, mumbling inaudibly while Alberto repeats his question. She responds, "Can I have five more minutes?" This approach allows the audience to infer her feelings based on her behavior.
Solution for Problem 2: Flesh out secondary characters by providing backstory and motivation. Introduce Alberto earlier in the screenplay, giving him a clear role and purpose within the narrative. This change creates a more consistent and engaging story world.
Solution for Problem 3: Integrate exposition into the narrative through subtle means such as visual cues, character actions, or brief flashbacks. Instead of relying solely on dialogue, find creative ways to weave necessary information into the fabric of the story, ensuring a smoother and more captivating viewing experience.
