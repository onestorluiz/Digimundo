DEPOIS: "[Sofia looks away, then turns back to Julio, her eyes shining.] I...I just knew, you know?"
→ Resultado esperado: Dialogue feels more authentic and engaging, enabling emotional investment in characters.
PROBLEMA 2: Minimal scene development (10 occurrences in 8 scenes)

→ Resultado esperado: Scenes become more immersive and engaging for the audience.
PROBLEMA 3: Forced exposition (6 occurrences in 5 scenes)

PROBLEMA 4: Lack of character development (10 occurrences in 8 scenes)

The four problems identified are interconnected, with on-the-nose dialogue often leading to minimal scene development and forced exposition, while lack of character development weakens the overall narrative. By addressing these issues, the screenplay will become more engaging, immersive, and emotionally resonant.
