## 🔑 KEY PRINCIPLES (DO NOT FORGET)

1. **Manual > Automated:** Manual reading identifies nuances grep/search miss
2. **Exact Terminology:** Use authors' exact words (e.g., "gap theory" not "conflict source")
3. **Interpret, Don't Extract:** Understand what concept MEANS, not just what it SAYS
4. **Cross-Cutting OK:** One concept (GAP) can apply to multiple specialists (conflict, tension, stakes)
5. **Focus Priority:** Specialists can overlap but must maintain primary focus (character specialist can touch dialogue but focuses on character)
6. **Quality > Speed:** Take time, understand deeply, apply precisely
7. **Work Continuously:** Don't ask for authorization, work systematically
8. **Document Everything:** Update checkpoint after each session
